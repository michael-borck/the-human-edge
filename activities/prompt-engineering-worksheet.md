# Exercise 2 — Prompt Workshop (digital workbook: exercises.html)

Pick 2 of 3 scenarios. Work on laptops; the workbook is the worksheet. No paper version.

| Round | Time | Do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Baseline | 10 min | Natural prompt. Rate the output honestly. |
| 2 RTCF | 20 min | Role, Task, Context, Format. Compare. |
| 3 Weaker-model ladder | 15 min | Add a worked example and an explicit constraint ("do not invent…"). Split into steps. Then fact-gate: *"Only change your assessment if I give you new facts, and name the fact that changed it."* Push back with no facts — does it fold or hold? |
| Debrief | 10 min | Name the component that changed output most. Save your best prompt to your library. |

**Why the ladder matters:** strong models forgive a sloppy brief because they infer what you left out. Weaker and free-tier models cannot. RTCF, examples, constraints, and one-step-at-a-time decomposition are non-negotiable there — and improve strong-model output too.

Scenarios: A) Difficult client email (GreenLeaf/David Chen). B) Board summary from a long document. C) Status update from raw notes. Full text in the digital workbook.
