# Exercise 3 — Data Challenge (digital workbook: exercises.html)

Dataset: `retailflow-customer-service-data.csv` (260 tickets, Jul–Dec 2024), downloaded from the workbook.

| Phase | Time |
|---|---|
| Upload and orient (fresh chat) | 5 min |
| Q1–Q2 progressive questioning | 15 min |
| Verification Challenge: three fluent claims, at least one wrong — verdict against the data | 10 min |
| Q3 recommendations and one-page brief to Karen | 10 min |
| Debrief with verdict reveal | 10 min |

Questions: top drivers of dissatisfaction; when response times blow out; what to recommend. Verify from outside the tool — against the data, never by asking the model if it is sure.
