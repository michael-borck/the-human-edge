# Series note

| Course | Question | Audience |
|---|---|---|
| 1. The Human Edge: Using and Delivering AI with Judgement (this repo) | How do I use AI in my work, with judgement? | All professionals |
| 2. AI-Driven Business Innovation | Which AI project should we fund? | Executives, strategists |
| 3. AI Leadership & Project Management | How do I deliver this AI project? | Project managers, team leads |

The full broken-ladder argument — structured coaching that makes quality standards explicit, comparative drills run as team practice, deciding which foundational skills to preserve as AI takes junior work — is leadership material for Courses 2 and 3. Course 1 carries it only as an opening plant and a closing thread (Decide · Practise · Trade · Revisit), because its audience must first be able to judge AI output themselves.

Framework lineage into university units (ISYS6014/ISYS6020) was previously tracked in COURSE-ALIGNMENT-MAP.md; that mapping now belongs with the unit outlines, which are the source of truth.
