# Series note (updated after the June 2026 merge)

Executive Education merged the three AI Capability Lifecycle courses into two. Practice (Course 1) and Delivery (Course 3) were both aimed at the individual user and risked splitting a thin market; Strategy (Course 2) has the organisational view. Steve Brown approved the combined title and looped in Genevieve Lawton to coordinate promotion.

| Offering | Question | Audience |
|---|---|---|
| **The Human Edge: Using and Delivering AI with Judgement** (this repo; combines AI in Practice + AI in Delivery) | How do I use AI with judgement, at my desk and across a project? | All professionals, then team leads |
| **AI in Strategy: Choosing Where to Invest** (was AI-Driven Business Innovation / AI to Drive Business Innovation) | Which AI project should we fund? | Executives, strategists |

The Ethics of AI masterclass already in the ExecEd program complements both at depth. Ethics, governance, and privacy stay threaded through this course at working level. It is a related offering to point at, not part of this pair.

## One idea, two scales

Approved combined shape for future Human Edge runs: morning builds judgement at desk scale (direct AI, judge trust, redesign a workflow); afternoon lifts the same judgement to delivery scale (scope, lead, and ship an AI project, human checkpoints designed in from the start). Leave with decision tools, a redesigned workflow, and a project plan.

**This run is desk scale in full.** It was booked as Building Everyday Capability, and it delivers that promise. The delivery-scale afternoon (pilot scoping, human checkpoints, scale/pivot/kill calls, a project-plan artefact) is approved direction, not built material. Do not sell it as part of this run. The deck close says exactly that: today at your desk, project scale is where this course goes next.

The full broken-ladder leadership material (structured coaching, comparative drills as team practice, which foundational skills to preserve) belongs to that delivery-scale material. Desk-scale runs carry it only as the closing thread: Decide, Practise, Trade, Revisit.
