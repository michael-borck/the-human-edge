Run of show. The Human Edge (one day)

Present from content/deck.html (presenter copy with notes: N toggles notes, 1 to 4 jump chapters). Participants get the published participant deck and the workbook at michael-borck.github.io/ai-in-practice. Site QR is on the title slide, the Exercise 1 slide, and as a URL on the close. The site is the handout.

The day at a glance

Time Block Slides The move that matters
9:00 Title, Today, introductions 1 to 2 Title strikeout in three beats. Site QR up while you talk. Show of hands A to D (hardly used it, played, most days, built into my work) and pace off the split. Round-the-room names and roles inside this block, fast.
9:15 Judge, teach 3 to 6 Tuition, your alarm (Gell-Mann), Trust Tool, Spectrum. At 9:35 stop for five minutes in pairs: plot one task from your actual week on the Trust Tool out loud. Hands move before any long talk does.
9:50 Exercise 1, compare drill 7 to 8 Five silent minutes for their own pass. Nobody opens AI first. Say the IP line before the first paste.
10:30 Morning tea Fix account lockouts now, not later. Note beginners for pairing.
11:00 Brief, teach 9 to 12 Conversation not delegation, RTCF, live before/after on the projector, weaker-model ladder with sycophancy named.
11:30 Exercise 2, prompt workshop 13 to 14 Two of three scenarios. Rounds 1 to 3, then the voice pass. Library entries start here.
12:30 Lunch Leave one question on screen worth arguing about: which task in your job stays human no matter what?
1:15 Verify, teach 15 to 16 Precision is never about right. VET it. Then the six steps.
1:40 Exercise 3, data challenge 17 to 18 Fresh chat. Verification Challenge sits between Q2 and Q3 on purpose. Vote before each verdict reveal.
2:30 Afternoon tea
3:00 Redesign, teach 19 to 21 Beyond chat plus memory safeguards, Opportunity Finder with sequencing, agents and skills.
3:20 Exercise 4, your workflow 22 Repeat the IP line for real work, sanitised. One step gets tested live. No exceptions.
4:00 Close, ladder and four moves 23 to 24 Three minutes only. The full leadership version belongs to Courses 2 and 3. Say so.
4:10 Action plan 25 Ten quiet minutes. Specific task, tool, day. Two volunteers read the week line aloud.
4:22 Close 26 Workbook export first, feedback QR second, hold the slide until phones go down.

The cut plan. Decide now, not on stage

  • Behind at lunch: the voice pass becomes tonight's homework in the workbook. Rounds 1 to 3 carry Brief without it.
  • Behind at 3:00: cut the Agents and Skills slide to one spoken sentence ("an agent automates the loop, not the standard, details in the reference") and go straight to Exercise 4.
  • Behind at 4:00: the ladder slide gets 60 seconds. The four moves still get their beats.
  • Never cut: the silent own-pass five minutes, the Verification Challenge, the live test in Exercise 4, the quiet action-plan time, or the export and feedback hold at the close. Those five carry the marketed outcomes.

The 20-minute dry run (day before, or morning of)

Done once, these prove the beats that can actually fail in the room.

  • Upload the RetailFlow CSV to a free ChatGPT, a free Claude, and Gemini from the venue network or your best guess of it. Note any upload cap or refusal. The workbook fallback (paste 50 rows, or describe the columns) is your plan B. Mean it.
  • Run the Before/After demo prompts once. Confirm the generic one still produces filler and the RTCF one still lands.
  • Scan both QR codes (site and feedback) from the back of the room, or your lounge at similar distance and light.
  • Open the workbook, type in three fields including a workflow row and a library row, export, and read the file. Then reload the page and confirm the typing survived.
