Personal Action Plan — The Human Edge

Completed in the digital workbook (exercises.html, final section) and exported. No paper version.

Decide. Practise. Trade. Revisit.

  • This week — Practise: one compare drill on a task that matters; one prompt saved to your library. Specific task, specific tool, specific day.
  • This month — Trade: redesign one workflow; run the AI-Assisted Analysis Workflow on one real dataset; hand over only what AI has proven reliable on your work.
  • This quarter — Decide + Revisit: name what must still be judged by human eyes in your role; calendar a revisit — models improve, stakes change, the line moves.
