AI for professionals: building everyday capability

Everyone in the room has the same AI tools. What sets you apart is knowing what to ask, when to trust the answer, and where a person stays in charge. This site has everything from the day: the tools, the exercises, the data, the prompt builder and the slides.

The day

Four sessions, one tool each. A few slides, then you do it, all day. Bring a laptop with at least one AI tool you’re allowed to use at work.

01 · 9:00

Judge

How much should I trust this output? Trust tool. You leave with a ten-second habit for every output.

02 · 11:00

Brief

How do I ask for what I need? RTCF. You leave with your own prompt library.

03 · 1:15

Check

How do I know it’s right? Check loop. You leave with an analysis you have verified yourself.

04 · 3:00

Redesign

Where does it fit in my week? Opportunity Finder. You leave with a redesigned workflow and an action plan.
