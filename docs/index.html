The Human EdgeUsing and delivering AI with judgement
Same day. Better name.

Everyone in the room has the same AI tools. What sets you apart is knowing what to ask, when to trust the answer, and where a person stays in charge, at your own desk and when you put AI into real work. This site has everything from the day: the tools, the exercises, the data, the prompts and the slides.

The day

Four sessions, one tool each. A few slides, then you do it, all day. Bring a laptop with at least one AI tool you’re allowed to use at work.

01 · 9:00

Judge

How much should I trust this output? Trust tool. You leave with a ten-second habit for every output.

02 · 11:00

Brief

How do I ask for what I need? RTCF. You leave with your own prompt library.

03 · 1:15

Check

How do I know it’s right? Check loop. You leave with an analysis you have verified yourself.

04 · 3:00

Deliver

Will it work every week? Opportunity Finder and a 30-day gate. You leave with a delivery plan for one real workflow, and an action plan.
