Digital workbook

This page replaces every paper worksheet. It saves automatically in this browser on this device — no account, nothing leaves your laptop. If wifi drops after this page has loaded, keep working. Export before you leave.

Privacy: do not paste confidential or personal data into free-tier AI tools during these exercises. Use the scenarios and dataset provided.

EXERCISE 1 · JUDGE · 35 MIN

The compare drill

Task: A strong performer of three years has been late and missing deadlines for a month. The team has noticed. Sketch your plan to address it.

EXERCISE 2 · BRIEF · 55 MIN

Prompt workshop — pick 2 of 3 scenarios

A. Difficult client email: GreenLeaf Industries says your solid deliverable is "too generic" after a board meeting you were not at. Respond: acknowledge without conceding poor work, propose a 1-hour alignment meeting. Contact: David Chen, VP Operations, 18-month relationship.

B. Board summary: Turn a long document (paste one of your own non-confidential documents, or use the pre-reading) into 5 board-ready bullet points with one decision requested.

C. Status update from raw notes: Notes: "API vendor late again, now Mar 15. Testing 80% done. Sarah back Monday. Budget 4% over, mostly contractor. Client demo went well." Produce a 200-word update: progress, risks, next steps.

Add one worked example and one explicit constraint ("do not invent…"). Then try the fact-gated guardrail: "Only change your assessment if I give you new facts, and name the fact that changed it." Push back with no new facts. Did it fold or hold?

Paste about 100 words of something you actually wrote (an old email or report paragraph, sanitised). Then ask: "Rewrite your last output in my voice, leading with the one thing only I would say." Compare. Generic competence sounds like everyone. This is how output starts sounding like you.

Ask the tool to summarise your style from that sample into a short style brief. Paste that brief into your AI tool's custom instructions or memory settings tonight, and every future draft starts closer to your voice.

EXERCISE 3 · VERIFY · 50 MIN

Data challenge — RetailFlow

Download the dataset (260 tickets, July to December). Start a fresh chat. Upload blocked or capped? Paste the first 50 rows as text, or describe the columns and row count and say so in your answers. The exercise still works, and noting the limit is part of verification. Orientation first: ask the tool to describe rows, columns, and date range — then check that description yourself.

A: "Email is about 3x slower than live chat."   B: "Account Issues has the lowest satisfaction and is the most complex category."   C: "Sunday response times are more than double the overall average." At least one is wrong.

EXERCISE 4 · REDESIGN · 40 MIN

Your AI-enhanced workflow

Real work, sanitised. A free chatbot is someone else's cloud. Use your task's shape and steps, change names and figures, and never paste client, personal, or confidential material. If a step is sensitive, describe it instead of pasting it, and follow your employer's approved-tool policy.

My prompt library — for my role

The scenarios were practice. This is the marketed take-home: prompts for the work you repeat. Draft at least three today (Exercise 4 will surface candidates), aim for five by the end of your first week back. Keep each prompt copyable as one block.

Personal action plan
