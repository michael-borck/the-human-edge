Free tools to explore

  • Google NotebookLM: paste in your sources (a PDF, a web page, your notes) and ask questions grounded in them, or get a briefing document. A good way to feel the difference between source-grounded and general answers.
  • Google Teachable Machine: train a small image or sound classifier in your browser in two minutes. Machine learning beyond chat, from the “bigger pie” slide.
  • Survival of the Best Fit: a 10-minute game in which an “objective” hiring algorithm amplifies the bias in its data.
  • Style Mirror: give it a sample of your writing and it produces a style brief for your custom instructions.
  • CiteSight: check references and catch the ones that don’t exist. VET’s “verify” step, for citations.
  • The trust tool game: ten scenarios per domain, with an explanation on every card.

Responsible AI guidance (Australia)

  • Guidance for AI Adoption: the Australian Government’s six essential practices for responsible AI use (October 2025), with an AI policy template.
  • Australia’s AI Ethics Principles: the earlier eight principles, now folded into the guidance above.
