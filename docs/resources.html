Free tools to explore

  • The trust tool game: ten scenarios per domain, with an explanation on every card.
  • Style Mirror: give it a sample of your writing and it produces a style brief for your custom instructions.
  • CiteSight: check references and catch the ones that don’t exist. VET’s “verify” step, for citations.
  • Google NotebookLM: ask questions grounded in sources you provide. A good way to feel the difference between source-grounded and general answers.
  • Msty Studio: runs AI models on your own laptop, or online models through your own API key. One install, and the free plan covers this course. The plainest way to see a system prompt, and to keep sensitive drafts off the cloud.
  • Ollama: the engine many local AI apps run on. Open WebUI and LibreChat are chat interfaces an IT team can host in front of it.
  • Google Teachable Machine: train a small image or sound classifier in your browser. Machine learning beyond chat.
  • Survival of the Best Fit: a 10-minute game in which an “objective” hiring algorithm amplifies the bias in its data.
