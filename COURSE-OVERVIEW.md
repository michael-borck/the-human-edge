# The Human Edge: Using and Delivering AI with Judgement

**One-Day Executive Education Workshop | Curtin Business School**
Marketed as *AI for Professionals: Building Everyday Capability* (same outcomes; new title announced in-room by the deck's opening strikeout).

A hands-on BYOD workshop: brief AI well, judge how far to trust it, verify data work from outside the tool, and redesign one real workflow. Participants leave with a prompt library, a verified one-page brief, a before/after workflow redesign, and a week/month/quarter action plan — all in one digital workbook they export on the day. No paper handouts.

**Spine:** the Trust Tool (Average/Precise × Small/Large). **Four tools:** Suitability Spectrum, RTCF (+ weaker-model ladder), Analysis Workflow, Opportunity Finder. **Four loops:** Judge, Brief, Verify, Redesign — see README.md for timings.

**Audience:** professionals in any function. **Prerequisites:** laptop, an account on one AI tool (checked in the 72-hour reminder), pre-reading read. **Size:** 15–30, tables of 4–6.

Offerings: this masterclass plus AI in Strategy: Choosing Where to Invest. Practice and Delivery were merged into The Human Edge (desk scale this run, project scale in future combined runs). See SERIES.md.
